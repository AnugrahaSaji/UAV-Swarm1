# Professor Review Prep

## Purpose
Prepare a high-signal review package for faculty or senior research feedback.

## Focus Areas
- claim defensibility
- novelty framing
- code-to-paper alignment
- evidence gaps
- section-level clarity and ordering
- likely reviewer attacks

## Workflow
1. Lock the draft under review and the active evidence scope.
2. Read the contribution framing and limitations sections first.
3. Identify claims that are strong, weak, or overstated.
4. Compare system-design claims against `core/` and `sscheduler/`.
5. Compare quantitative claims against canonical artifacts.
6. Produce a short professor-facing review brief.

## Review Rules
- Prefer “this needs narrower wording” over speculative criticism.
- Separate:
  - correctness risks
  - evidence risks
  - framing risks
  - readability risks
- If a draft is between versions, state what changed between the versions that matters to reviewer confidence.

## Output
Produce a compact brief with:
- `submission_target`
- `strongest_contributions`
- `claims_to_narrow`
- `claims_requiring_new_evidence`
- `sections_professors_will_question`
- `recommended_review_order`
- `suggested_questions_for_profs`
